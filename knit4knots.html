Knit4Knots

Overview

Knit4Knots is a personalized e-commerce web application and digital storefront created for an independent handmade crochet business. Originally conceptualized as a creative brand in 2018, the project evolved into a fully functional online platform designed to showcase, manage, and sell unique, handcrafted crochet garments and accessories.

Objective

To transition a long-standing creative brand (founded in 2018) from a concept into a professional, scalable digital marketplace. The primary goals were to design a cozy, artisanal aesthetic that reflects the handmade nature of the products, ensure seamless cross-device responsiveness for shoppers, and establish a secure, streamlined e-commerce experience for processing orders and inventory.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend Customization: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (used for modifying storefront templates, crafting custom layouts, and refining UI/UX styling).
  • Social Media & Marketing: Instagram and Facebook brand management, content strategy, and community engagement.
  • E-Commerce Platform & CMS Big Cartel (Hosted SaaS platform managing products, inventory variants, and order fulfillment).
  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Managed Cloud Hosting via Big Cartel (Includes automated SSL security, CDN distribution, and server maintenance).
  • Payment Gateways: Integrated Stripe & PayPal for secure client transactions.
  • Domain & DNS: Custom domain configuration (www.knit4knots.com).

Key Features

  • Brand Heritage & Identity: Built on a creative foundation established in 2018, blending years of brand identity refinement into a polished web presentation.
  • Social Media Management: Direct curation of Instagram and Facebook channels, showcasing product releases, custom curations, and building a loyal follower base.
  • Artisanal Storefront Design: Customized typography, color schemes, and layout styling using CSS3 to evoke a warm, welcoming, and high-end handmade feel.
  • Product Catalog & Pricing Variants: Organized product listings featuring detailed descriptions, pricing variations, and live inventory tracking for unique items.
  • Responsive Mobile Layout: Optimized grid structures and media queries ensuring smooth navigation, clear text readability, and easy checkout across small phones, tables, and desktops.
  • Secure Checkout & SSL Integration: Integrated third-party payment processing paired with HTTPS security for a trusted, frictionless customer purchasing flow.
